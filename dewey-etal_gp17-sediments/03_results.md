## Results

### Elemental concentrations 
![[figure_elemental_depth_profiles.png]]
**Figure 2.** Depth profiles of major and minor element concentrations in sediment cores from three GP17-ANT stations. (a, c, e) XRF-derived concentrations of Fe, Ti, Ca, and Si. (b, d, f) CHNS-derived concentrations of C, N, and S. Error bars represent ±1σ of replicate measurements. (a, b) Station 15 (core 19908); (c, d) Station 27 (core 20892); (e, f) Station 5 (core 19119). Stations span a transect across the Antarctic continental margin.

Fe and Ti concentrations varied modestly across stations, whereas Ca showed large inter-station differences (Figure 2). Fe was highest and most uniform at Station 27 (18.8-20.0 wt%, mean 19.4 wt%), lower and more variable at Station 15 (15.9-20.1 wt%, mean 17.2 wt%), and lowest at Station 5 (11.0-16.9 wt%, mean 14.7 wt%). Ti followed a similar pattern, averaging 2.7 wt% at Station 27 versus ~1.7-1.8 wt% at Stations 5 and 15. Ca was substantially elevated in shallow sediments at Station 5 (32.5 wt% at 1 cm, 31.3 wt% at 3 cm) but dropped sharply to 8.5 wt% at 5 cm and <5 wt% below, while Stations 15 and 27 had low, uniform Ca throughout (~2.7 and ~5.2 wt%, respectively). Si was relatively uniform across all stations and depths (32.7-46.8 wt%, mean 42.0 wt%).

C and N concentrations showed distinct depth structure at each station (Figure 2). At Station 5, C was elevated at 1 and 3 cm (2.45 and 2.87 wt%) but decreased sharply to 0.58 wt% at 5 cm and <0.24 wt% below. C decreased more gradually at Station 15 (1.17 wt% at 1 cm to ~0.9-1.0 wt% below) and at Station 27 (0.56 wt% at 3 cm to 0.27 wt% at 15 cm). N was uniformly low at Stations 27 and 5 (0.02-0.08 and 0.02-0.04 wt%, respectively) but at Station 15, N increased sharply below 7 cm, from 0.11-0.17 wt% in the upper core to 0.44-0.60 wt% at 9-15 cm. S was broadly similar across all cores (0.18-0.54 wt%), decreasing slightly with depth.

**Figure 8.** $^{57}$Fe Mössbauer spectra of Station 27 sediments. Gray circles show measured data; colored curves show fitted components. (a) Room-temperature (295 K) spectra at 1, 5, and 15 cm depth, fit with Fe(III) doublet (pink), Fe(II) doublet (green), and a magnetic sextet (orange) at 1 cm.
